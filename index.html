I’m a Robotics and AI undergraduate at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham in Chennai, India. I work on Model Predictive Control for autonomous UAVs: planning trajectories that track a goal, avoid obstacles and respect the vehicle’s limits.

What interests me most is control that is efficient as well as accurate. My battery-aware MPC project adds a jerk penalty and a battery state-of-charge model to the cost, which cut control energy by up to 25.3% and battery drain by up to 22.7% against a baseline MPC. I also build the hardware side: an autonomous drone on a CUAV X7+ flight controller running ArduPilot and custom Lua scripts.

Research

Battery-Aware Model Predictive Control for Autonomous UAVs

2026PythonCVXPYOSQPNumPy

A dual-loop MPC framework for quadrotor trajectory planning on a discrete-time state-space model. Each step solves a constrained quadratic program for tracking and obstacle avoidance. A jerk-aware cost and a battery state-of-charge model reduce aggressive inputs and save energy: across five mission profiles, up to 25.3% lower control energy, 57.6% lower jerk and 22.7% lower battery drain than baseline MPC. A prediction-horizon ablation and ±30% parameter sensitivity analysis test robustness and computational trade-offs.

Autonomous UAV Development and Waypoint Navigation

2026CUAV X7+ArduPilotMission PlannerLua

Designed and built an autonomous drone around the CUAV X7+ flight controller, configured and monitored through Mission Planner. It flies GPS waypoint missions and runs custom Lua scripts for autonomous operations.

Anomaly Detection in UAV Systems

2026Pythonscikit-learnpandasNumPy

A machine learning pipeline that flags anomalies in UAV flight and sensor data, covering feature engineering, preprocessing, classification and model evaluation for reliable system monitoring.
